Read this before trusting the numbers

  • The backtest is in-sample. Weights are chosen using the same window they're tested on, so the optimized portfolios look better than anyone could have done in real time. Max Sharpe especially piles into whatever happened to do best.
  • These are paper portfolios. French's returns carry no fees, trading costs, taxes or cash drag. Real funds in these categories earned less, most of all in small cap and emerging markets.
  • History gets restated. French rebuilds every series on each update, so numbers can shift slightly after a data refresh.
  • US Market is the total US market (Mkt-RF + RF). International returns are in USD. Cash is the 1-month T-bill, and Sharpe ratios are measured against it.
